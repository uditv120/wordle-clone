How To Play Lexicon Supreme

Master words across multiple game modes and level up your mastery!

🎮 Game Modes

  • Endless: Continuous rounds with zero repeats until word pool is cleared.
  • Daily Challenge: A synchronized word for every calendar day.
  • Time Rush: 60s speed challenge. Solving a word earns +15s bonus and builds combo streaks!
  • Duo Lexicon: Solve 2 hidden words simultaneously using the same keystrokes across 7 guesses. Keyboard keys split colors for both boards!
  • Challenge a Friend: Generate encrypted custom secret links to battle your friends!

🟩 Color Rules

W Green: Letter is in the word and in the exact spot.
I Yellow: Letter is in the word but wrong spot.
N Gray: Letter is not present in the word.

🪙 Power-Ups & Fortune Wheel

Spend coins earned from winning streaks to Scout green letters, Cull unused letters, or get Clue Hints. Don't forget to spin the 🎡 Lucky Wheel for daily rewards!
